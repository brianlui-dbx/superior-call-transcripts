# Genie One for Superior Plus Propane
### Retention-Aware Pricing — capture the pricing upside without buying it back in attrition

**Orchestration:** Native Genie One only · **Pillar:** Superior Delivers → Customer Growth (~$30M) · **Scope:** NY + NJ residential (demo)

---

**THE TENSION (in Superior's own words)**
2026 plan takes **pricing actions to grow margin** while facing **service pressure and attrition** during the transformation. A blanket price increase hits hardest exactly the accounts already wounded by a runout, a late delivery, or a long call wait — so you can erase the pricing win with the churn it triggers.

**THE DECISION GENIE ONE MAKES**
> Which accounts can absorb a price increase, and which must we protect — with evidence from every silo behind each name?

---

**Native-only synthesis:** cross-domain joins are **pre-computed in the pipeline** into one governed, account-grain `account_retention_snapshot` (aggregated *before* joining — no fan-out). Strategic questions route to the Retention space where the join already lives; the other three serve drill-down and evidence.

---

**THE "AHA"**  A rate objection **stacked on** a verified service failure = the churn bomb. No single system sees it — the snapshot does.

100K accounts · 2pt avoidable-churn reduction · $600 contribution/account · $150K program cost → **$1.05M net/yr** · break-even **0.25pt (250 accounts)**.
