# Glossary

Short definitions for terms that recur in this repository.

| Term | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Offer blocker | A reason a customer did not accept an offer. |
| Opportunity | One sales deal, identified by a Salesforce Opportunity ID. |
| Disposition | How much a finding affected the deal — from deal-killer to passing mention. |
| Qualifier | The sub-classification within a blocker code. |
